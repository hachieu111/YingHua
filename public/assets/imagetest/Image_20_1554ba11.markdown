## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a whimsical, luminous, and culturally grounded language-learning experience tailored for Vietnamese learners immersing themselves in Chinese (Mandarin) via digital manhua (comics). The design bridges modern East Asian visual storytelling with airy, joyful product interfaces.

### Core Philosophy
- **Approachable Immersion:** Language acquisition should feel like reading your favorite webtoon in a tranquil cafe, not studying a textbook.
- **Floral Serenity:** Subtle cherry blossom (樱花) motifs, petal-like curvatures, and translucent overlays evoke spring blooms, softness, and fresh beginnings.
- **Graphic Clarity:** Comic panels require high visual attention; the interface frames and supports reading with unobtrusive pastel tones, high-legibility slate typography, and gentle spatial depth.

### Design Movement
**Pastel Glassmorphism & Soft Modernism:** A hybrid aesthetic blending high-translucency frosted glass surfaces (`backdrop-filter: blur`), gentle pill-shaped components, warm ambient light, and organic curves. The interface remains feather-light and tactile without sliding into visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances vibrant pastels with a grounding slate architecture, engineered specifically for prolonged reading sessions and comic panel readability.

### Key Palettes
- **Canvas (`#FBF9FE`):** A soft, warm off-white with faint violet-rose undertones, reducing ocular strain compared to pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Rose Blossom (`#F472B6` / `#FBCFE8`):** The brand identity anchor used for active highlights, vocabulary bookmarks, call-to-action triggers, and progress tracking.
- **Secondary Sky Breeze (`#60A5FA` / `#93C5FD`):** Represents pinyin guides, pronunciation tooltips, grammar tags, and audio play states.
- **Tertiary Sunlight Yellow (`#FDE68A` / `#FEF08A`):** Used for streak indicators, HSK level achievements, review reminders, and contextual comic hints.
- **Text & Neutral Slate:**
  - `slate-900` (`#1E293B`): Primary body, hanzi characters, dialogue translation, and prominent titles.
  - `slate-500` (`#64748B`): Secondary text, pinyin tones, reading metadata, and non-active icons.
  - `slate-100` (`#F1F5F9`): Structural hairpins, translucent panel dividers, and subtle borders.

### Surface Color Mapping
- Glass cards leverage semi-transparent whites (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) sitting on `#FBF9FE` with a soft pink ambient blur behind high-priority reading widgets.

## Typography

The type hierarchy harmonizes Latin script (Vietnamese with full tone marks), Chinese Simplified Hanzi glyphs, and Pinyin orthography.

- **Headlines & Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Features rounded geometric terminals that evoke friendliness, youthful optimism, and cleanliness.
- **Body & Manhua Dialogues (`Inter`):** Selected for its neutral, highly optimized micro-legibility and full Unicode diacritic coverage, ensuring Vietnamese translations render without baseline jitters.
- **Chinese Glyph Pairing:** When rendering Hanzi alongside body or dialogue text, fallback cascades through system-native standard KaiTi/PingFang SC/Noto Sans SC, preserving uniform stroke weight relative to Inter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid optimized for continuous comic scrolling, vertical webtoon viewports, and side-by-side split interaction (comic reader on the left, vocabulary/notes drawer on the right).

### Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single flow. Margin `1rem`. Comics occupy full width edge-to-edge; translation overlays, word lookups, and Pinyin tooltips float over the comic via a bottom sheet drawer.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column hybrid layout. Margin `2rem`, gutter `1.5rem`. Reader accommodates comic viewport with interactive collapsible side panels for flashcards and vocabulary lists.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column fixed-maximum grid (`max-width: 1440px`). Center-staged manhua viewport with fixed contextual learning sidebars (audio controls, HSK breakdown, grammar analysis).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy, dark drop shadows. Instead, it employs **frosted glassmorphism**, soft border tints, and petal-tinted ambient backdrops.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Base background `#FBF9FE`.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Sub-cards):** Solid or 90% opaque `#FFFFFF` cards with a crisp `1px solid #F1F5F9` boundary and an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 2px 10px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Glass Cards & Popovers):** Translucent white `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Floating Reading Dictionaries):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.94)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, surrounded by a subtle rose-tinted glow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.18), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **pill-shaped (level 3)** geometry. Inspired by falling cherry blossom petals and the rounded forms of manhua speech bubbles, sharp edges are systematically eliminated.

- **Buttons, Badges, Search Inputs:** Fully curved pill geometries (`border-radius: 9999px` or `rounded-xl` minimum).
- **Cards & Readers:** Generous `2rem` (`32px`) corner radiuses that provide an organic, inviting frame to square comic panels.
- **Interactive Hanzi Tiles:** Rounded square pills (`rounded-lg`, `16px`) providing an optimal finger and mouse target for character tracing and vocabulary tap-to-translate.

## Components

All microcopy and interactive states below are localized into natural Vietnamese.

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary ("Bắt đầu học", "Tiếp tục đọc"):** Filled gradient from `#F472B6` to `#FB7185`, white text, pill radius (`9999px`), subtle shadow with pink tone. Hover states gently scale (1.02x) with an increased soft glow.
- **Secondary ("Luyện tập", "Xem từ vựng"):** Semi-transparent `#FBCFE8` at 30% opacity with `#F472B6` text and border `1px solid #FBCFE8`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary ("Bỏ qua", "Để sau"):** Transparent fill, slate-500 text, slate-100 hover background.

### 2. Chips & Badges
- **HSK Level Badges ("HSK 1", "HSK 2"):** Butter yellow background (`#FEF08A`), text `#854D0E`, rounded-full, with an optional mini blossom icon.
- **Grammar & Tone Badges ("Ngữ pháp", "Thanh 1"):** Sky blue tint (`#E0F2FE`), text `#0369A1`, pill radius.
- **Streak & Reward Chips ("7 ngày liên tục"):** Soft pink pill (`#FCE7F3`), text `#BE185D`.

### 3. Comic Interaction Cards
- **Speech Bubble Click-to-Translate:** Glassmorphism overlay appearing adjacent to the comic panel. Contains:
  - Hanzi in `24px` semi-bold.
  - Pinyin in `#60A5FA` font weight 600.
  - Vietnamese translation in slate-900 body.
  - Audio icon button ("Phát âm") with soft blue fill.

### 4. Input Fields & Search
- **Search ("Tìm truyện manhua, từ vựng..."):** Background `#FFFFFF`, rounded-full (`9999px`), border `1px solid #F1F5F9`, inner icon slate-400. On focus, outline blooms into `rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.4)` with `2px` focus ring.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Circular cherry blossom check states: when selected, circles fill with `#F472B6` featuring a crisp white inner checkmark.
- Radio options for quizzes ("Chọn đáp án đúng") use large pill cards that highlight with a sky-blue border (`#60A5FA`) and soft pastel background when active.

### 6. Specialized Components: Flashcard Deck
- Dual-sided glassmorphism card (`rounded-2xl`, `p-6`).
- Top-right bookmark indicator using a cherry blossom petal icon.
- Bottom actions: "Đã thuộc" (Soft sky blue pill) and "Học lại" (Soft pink pill).
